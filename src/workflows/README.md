# Workflows

Automated workflows and routines managed through Base44's workflow engine.

## Active Workflows

### Flag Calendar Conflicts
- **Trigger:** Google Calendar connector event
- **Action:** Detect double-bookings and notify the user

### Prep Your Meetings
- **Trigger:** Google Calendar connector event
- **Action:** Send meeting prep 30 minutes before each meeting

## Paused Workflows (credit-saving)

### Label Important Emails
- **Trigger:** Gmail connector event (new message)
- **Action:** Apply labels (Important Emails, Newsletters, Learning, Software Projects, Account Security)
- **Status:** Paused to reduce credit consumption

### Organize Recurring Topics
- **Trigger:** Gmail connector event (new message)
- **Action:** Categorize newsletters and promotional content
- **Status:** Paused to reduce credit consumption

## Inactive Workflows

### Send Your Morning Brief
- **Trigger:** Scheduled — daily at 8:00 AM (Africa/Lagos)
- **Action:** Compile calendar, security alerts, email updates, investing news, AI/tech news

### Pull Your Market Pulse
- **Trigger:** Scheduled — daily at 9:00 AM (Africa/Lagos)
- **Action:** Nigerian and global market insights (fixed income, bond yields, equities)

## Planned Workflows

### TikTok Weekly Stats Tracker
- **Trigger:** Scheduled — weekly
- **Action:** Pull follower count, video stats, and track growth over time

### Content Planning Pipeline
- **Trigger:** On-demand
- **Action:** Draft captions and hashtags for cross-platform content (Facebook + TikTok)
